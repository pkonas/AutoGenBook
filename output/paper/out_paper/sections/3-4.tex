The assembly stage deterministically transforms validated, JSON-constrained section outputs into a compilable LaTeX manuscript plus refs.bib: it maps JSON fields to LaTeX structure, deduplicates BibTeX entries, emits a top-level TeX wrapper preserving document-graph order, and retains provenance metadata.  
The mapping is conservative: free-text fields become paragraph bodies, the ``section_title'' field maps to \section/\subsection by node depth, and explicit ``citations'' arrays drive insertion of \cite{} tokens.  
Pre-assembly validation combines schema checks (required fields) with evidence-aware audits (citation coverage, BibTeX consistency, unsupported-claim heuristics); failures yield structured artifacts (error type, node key, offending fragment).  
A prioritized remediation policy attempts deterministic fixes (reject/regenerate malformed JSON, re-retrieve missing evidence, reconstruct or shortlist BibTeX records, re-run generation for inconsistent subtrees), logs every automated change with the prior JSON, action and rationale for human review; the procedures are conceptual and favor human-in-the-loop auditing.
