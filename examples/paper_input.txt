Title: Graph-Aware Prompting for Reliable Section Generation
Summary: We propose a graph-guided prompting workflow for long-form scientific writing. The method uses a document graph, strict JSON schemas, and evidence-aware prompts to produce consistent sections with citations.
Keywords: document graph, long-form generation, evidence, citations, structured prompting
Research questions: How does graph-guided sectioning affect coherence and citation quality?
Contributions: (1) graph-based section scheduler, (2) evidence injection for each section, (3) audit-friendly output format
Method: Build a section graph, retrieve relevant evidence, write leaf sections, and assemble LaTeX with refs.bib.
Experiments: Compare to baseline linear prompting on two topics, measure citation coverage and factual consistency.
Datasets: Internal notes and a small curated PDF set (if available).
Metrics: citation coverage, hallucination rate, reviewer score.
Notes: Keep claims conservative and always cite sources.
